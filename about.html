About

I lead UX and product design for B2B enterprise SaaS, and I'm at my best on version one: the design system that doesn't exist yet, the journey nobody owns, the feature in a domain new to everyone.

What draws me there is that version one is where the structure gets decided, and structure is what everything after it inherits. Get it right and the next five years are cheaper. Get it wrong and every team after you pays for it quietly.

Strategy isn't done until it ships. I'm most interested in the work that compounds: a design system that outlives its first release, a team set up so nobody solves the same problem twice, AI that gets me fluent in a new domain in days instead of months.

I started in industrial design, and it never quite left. I still think about interfaces the way I think about objects. They have constraints, tolerances, and consequences, and someone has to live with all three.

Along the way: seven international design awards, and a design system that's still about 80% in use eight years later.
